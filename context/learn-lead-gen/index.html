2 · Clicks vs leads

You pay for every click.
Only some of them call.

Ten people click the ad at $8 each. On a good page, about one of them calls. So that one call really cost $80. That's the cost per lead: a number you calculate, not a separate bill.

5 · Wasted clicks

What if people click but never call?

That's money gone, and stopping it is your real job. Your main tool is the negative keyword list: words that block bad searches before they cost anything.

Negative keywords at work

blocked: jobs · free · diy · how to · salary

Green searches reach the ad. Red ones are blocked and cost $0.

The budget is a hard cap

You set a daily budget. When it's used, the ad stops until tomorrow. A busy day can go up to 2×, but the month never goes over daily × 30.4.

$0 / $100 today

Fake clicks get refunded

Bots, double-clicks and competitors count as invalid clicks. Google filters most of them and doesn't charge.

Going "viral" is free

People sharing the website link cost nothing. You only pay for clicks on the ad itself.

7 · The guarantee

Hit the number, or they walk

Before launch you write the lead number down together. The 90-day clock starts when the ads go live.

Day 0agree on the number, ads live within 14 days
Month 1Google learns, leads are slow
Month 2block waste, leads speed up
Day 90count qualified leads
✓ Hit itThe 6-month plan continues. Your numbers become your first case study.
✗ Missed itThe client can cancel early and keep their site and ad account. You refund nothing.

What counts as a lead

A tracked call of 60+ seconds, or a real form. Not spam, not job seekers, not existing customers.

The client's side

Keep the agreed ad budget, don't pause the ads, and answer calls and forms during business hours.

On-time guarantee

Live in 14 days, every lead tracked, report by the 5th, or that month is free. This one costs real money, so protect your dates.
