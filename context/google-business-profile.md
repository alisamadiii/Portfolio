## Q&A seeds (ask from a personal account, answer from the business)

1. **Q:** Is ad spend included in your monthly price?
   **A:** No. Google bills ad spend to your card directly, so you see every dollar. Our monthly fee covers managing the campaigns, the website, and lead tracking.
2. **Q:** Do you only build websites?
   **A:** We're a lead generation agency. Most clients get Google Ads plus a website built to convert those leads, and we also offer a website-only plan if you don't want ads yet.
3. **Q:** Do I own my accounts if I leave?
   **A:** Yes. Your Google Ads account, domain, and website code are in your name from day one.
4. **Q:** Do you offer a guarantee?
   **A:** Yes. Before launch we agree in writing on how many qualified leads your campaigns should bring in. If we miss it within 90 days, you can cancel the 6-month minimum and keep your website and Google Ads account. Site and ads go live within 14 days and your report arrives by the 5th of each month, or that month is free.
