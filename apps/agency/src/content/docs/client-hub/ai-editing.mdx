The fastest way to change your website is to ask. Open **Website** in the
sidebar (or click **Edit with AI** anywhere) and you get a chat on the left and
a live preview of your site on the right. You describe a change, the AI makes
it in the preview, and nothing reaches your real website until you click
**Publish**.

Above the preview you can switch pages (**Pages**), switch between desktop,
tablet and phone views, **Reload** the preview, open it in a new tab
(**Preview**), and check each page's search title and description (**SEO**).

## Make a change in 4 steps

1. **Point at it.** Click the cursor button at the top right of the preview,
   then click the text, image or button you want to change. It shows up above
   the message box, so the AI knows exactly what you mean.
2. **Describe the change** in plain words, like texting a designer:
   "Change this headline to 'Jacksonville's friendly, same-day plumbers'".
3. **Send** with Enter (Shift+Enter adds a new line). Watch the preview update.
   While the AI works, the send button turns into a pause button.
4. **Publish** when you're happy. Until then, everything stays in the preview.

## How to ask so it works the first time

- **Click the element before you type.** It removes all guessing.
- **One clear change per message** beats a long list.
- **Name the page or section** when the element isn't on screen: "On the
  About page, add a fourth team member card".
- **Quote the exact text** when there are several similar things: "the button
  that says Get a quote", not "the button".
- **Say "everywhere"** to change something across the whole site, like a new
  phone number.
- **"Do the same for..."** repeats your last request somewhere else.

If your request matches several things on the page, the AI asks which one you
mean instead of guessing. If it's too vague ("make it pop"), it asks what
exactly to change. Click **Learn about AI** at the top of the chat for a quick
summary any time.

## What the AI can and can't do

**It can:**

- Change text, headings, buttons and labels anywhere.
- Add, remove or reorder sections and list items on a page.
- Add new entries, like a team member, an FAQ, a project or an event.
- Change colors, spacing and styling on what you point to.
- Update titles and descriptions for search engines.
- Place images you upload.

**It can't:**

- Undo from memory. Use **Changes** to revert (see below).
- Crop or reposition inside a photo. Crop it first, then upload it.
- Redesign a whole page, or create and delete pages, unless your site has a
  skill for it (see below).
- Wire up payments, logins or new form behavior. It can add a link to a
  checkout or booking page you give it.
- Change site settings or billing.

For bigger projects like a redesign or a new feature, [contact us](/contact).

## Adding images

Click the paperclip, drag images into the chat, or paste them.

- Up to **10 images** per message, **1 MB** each. Large photos are shrunk
  automatically, so most phone photos fit.
- JPG, PNG, WebP and GIF work. Export iPhone HEIC photos as JPG first. SVG
  files aren't supported here.
- **Add a short description to each image** before sending, like "new team
  photo for the About page". The AI places images by that description.
- Videos can't be uploaded. Host them on YouTube or Vimeo and paste the link.

## Skills: shortcuts for your site

Some sites come with **skills**, ready-made tasks we set up for your site,
like "add a page", "update business info", "manage FAQs" or "add an event".
Type **/** in the message box to see them, or click a skill chip above the
box. If you pick a skill without details, the AI asks one short question
first, then does the work. Skills can do things normal chat can't, like
creating a new page wired into your menu and sitemap.

Want a skill for something you do often? Ask us.

## Changes, revert and discard

**Changes** at the top shows every file that differs from your live site, with
removed lines in red and added lines in green. **Revert** puts one file back
exactly as it is live.

Typing "undo" in the chat doesn't work: the AI doesn't remember old content.
Use **Changes** instead.

**Discard** throws away every change from this session and resets the preview
to your live site.

## Publishing

Click **Publish** to put everything in the preview on your real website. Only
owners and full access members see the button.

- **Sites on GitHub:** your changes are saved to GitHub and your host updates
  the live site, usually within a minute or two.
- **Uploaded sites:** the first time, the Hub asks you to connect your own
  Netlify account, which hosts the site. After that, Publish deploys straight
  to Netlify, and the **Netlify** page in the sidebar shows the deploy.
- **Sites we manage for you:** the Hub saves your changes and our team puts
  them live.

You can't publish while the AI is still working on a request. After
publishing, a fresh preview starts automatically.

## Branches: try ideas side by side

The branch chip above the preview (it says **main** by default) lets you
create a named branch, like "New hero test", to try changes without touching
your main version. Switching branches starts a new session on that branch.
Publish or discard your changes on main before switching. Uploaded sites don't
have branches.

## Good to know

- The editor goes to sleep after about 30 minutes without activity. Publish
  when you finish a batch of changes, then open it again whenever you need it.
- Each message has up to 10 minutes to finish.
- If the editor is busy, wait a minute and try again.
- To replace your whole uploaded site with new files, use **Import** on Home.
  Unpublished editor changes are discarded, and your live site keeps the old
  version until you publish or redeploy.
