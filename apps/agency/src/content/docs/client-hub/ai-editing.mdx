The fastest way to change your website is to just ask. The Hub gives you AI in
two places, and they fit different jobs:

1. **Quick edits**: click any element on the page, tell the AI what to change,
   and it happens in the background while you keep working.
2. **Chat sessions**: a full conversation with a live preview of your site,
   for bigger changes you want to watch happen.

## Quick edits

For small changes, you don't need a conversation. Click any text element in
the editor, type what you want ("make this friendlier", "update the hours to
9-5"), and send it. The AI applies the change in the background. You can close
the tab and move on with your day.

Every quick edit is tracked in the **Deployments** tab. Any request you send
outside a chat session shows up there with all its data: what was asked, what
the AI changed, and the resulting publish. Deployments only cover these
background edits; chat sessions don't appear there because you already watch
those live.

Because quick edits run in the background (you're not watching them live), the
Hub tells you by **email** if something couldn't be applied. The email shows
exactly what you asked for, why it didn't apply, and what to do next: rephrase
the request, or reach out to us. If a change is rejected, nothing on your site
is touched.

## Chat sessions

For bigger changes, start an **AI session**: a chat sidebar next to a live
preview of your actual site.

*A preview video of chat sessions will be ready soon.*

1. Open your project in the Hub and start an AI session.
2. A live preview of your site loads on the left, with a chat sidebar on the
   right.
3. Type what you want, the way you'd tell a person:
   - *"Change the headline on the homepage to 'Serving Jacksonville since
     2019'."*
   - *"Add a new section under services about kitchen remodeling."*
   - *"Make the photos on the About page bigger."*
4. The AI makes the change and the preview updates in front of you. Not quite
   right? Keep talking (*"a bit smaller"*, *"use a warmer tone"*) until it is.

### You see everything live before publishing

When you enable a chat session, you get a **preview URL**: your own private
copy of the site where every change shows up live as the AI works. Open it on
your phone, share it with a colleague, poke around. Your visitors never see
any of it.

That's the point of a session: you watch every change with your own eyes, so
when you press **Publish**, you do it freely and with confidence. Your changes
go to the live site, usually within a minute.

## What if I break something?

You can't. Three safety nets:

- Chat sessions happen on a preview that's completely separate from your live
  site, so experiments cost nothing.
- Quick edits that can't be applied are rejected and change nothing; you get
  an email explaining why.
- Every published change is saved in version control, so we can restore any
  previous version of your site at any time.

## Tips for good requests

- **One change at a time** works better than a list of ten.
- **Point at the page**: "on the homepage", "in the footer", "the pricing
  section".
- **Paste the exact text** you want when wording matters.
