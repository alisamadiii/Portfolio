The **Uber Eats** app shows what your Uber Eats store is doing: today's
orders, the last 30 days of sales and every order as it comes in. It also
has a **Pause orders** button for when the kitchen can't keep up.

You keep accepting orders the way you do now, on your Uber Eats tablet or in
the Uber Eats Orders app, unless you turn on **Accept orders in Client Hub**
(see below). Orders from every delivery app are also listed together under
[Orders](/docs/client-hub/orders).

## Connecting your store

1. In Client Hub, go to **Apps** and add **Uber Eats**.
2. Press **Connect Uber Eats** and sign in with the Uber account that manages
   your restaurant. Uber asks whether Client Hub may see your stores. You
   never share your password with us.
3. If that account manages more than one store, pick the one this website is
   for. Each store connects to its own project.

That's it. New orders show up within a minute of being placed, and the page
refreshes on its own while it's open.

## What you see

- **Your store**: the name and address Uber lists, whether it's accepting
  orders right now, and a link to your store page on Uber Eats.
- **Orders today / 7 days / Sales, 30 days / Average order**: counted from the
  orders Client Hub has seen. Canceled and denied orders aren't counted as
  sales. Until the first order arrives, these show "—".
- **Orders**: the order number your tablet shows, when it was placed, the
  items, the total and its status (New, In progress, Completed, Canceled).

We save the customer's first name, what they ordered and the totals. No
phone numbers or addresses are kept.

## Order details and receipt

Click any order to open it. Client Hub reads it fresh from Uber Eats, so
you see the items with their options and the customer's notes, the full
price breakdown, and, for Uber deliveries, the **courier**: name, photo,
car and plate, where they are, when they'll arrive, and a number to call
them. Uber assigns the courier after you accept the order on your tablet, so
the card fills in a minute or two later.

**Print receipt** prints just the receipt (store, order number, items,
totals, courier name and plate) on a normal or 80 mm receipt printer. It
also reads fine on a phone when the courier asks which order is theirs.

Courier and customer details are shown live and not saved.

## Accepting orders in Client Hub

By default you keep accepting orders on your Uber Eats tablet and Client Hub
only watches. If you'd rather accept from Client Hub too, turn on **Accept
orders in Client Hub** on the store card. Client Hub then becomes the
store's order manager on Uber Eats:

- New orders show an **Accept** button in the list and **Accept / Deny** in
  the order, with a clock. Uber cancels any order nobody accepts within
  about 11 minutes, so keep the page or the tablet in view during service.
- Denying asks for a reason (item not available, too busy, closed…) and a
  short note; Uber passes it to the customer.
- Anyone with editor access or higher on the project can accept and deny.
  Only the owner can turn the switch on or off.

Turn it off anytime to go back to tablet-only.

## What you earn

Uber Eats doesn't send its commission in the order feed, so Client Hub shows
two things:

- **What Uber reports**: the food subtotal, any promotion you funded, the
  tax you remit yourself, fees paid out to you, tips, and (for orders you
  deliver yourself) Uber's marketplace fee.
- **An estimate**: enter your Uber commission rate once on the store card
  (it's in your Uber Eats contract). Client Hub then shows an estimated Uber
  fee and estimated payout for every order and for the last 30 days. The
  estimate takes the commission on the subtotal after your promotions, then
  adds the tax you remit, fees paid to you and tips.

Exact payouts always come from **Uber Eats Manager › Payments**. Treat the
numbers here as a quick read, not an accounting record.

## Pausing and resuming

**Pause orders** takes your store offline on Uber Eats so no new orders come
in. Pick how long (30 minutes, an hour, two hours, the rest of today, or until
you resume) and an optional reason that only Uber sees. Orders already in
progress aren't affected. **Resume orders** puts the store back online right
away. Pausing from the Uber Eats tablet works the same; Client Hub shows
whichever state Uber reports.

## Disconnecting

**Disconnect and delete saved orders** unlinks the store and deletes the
orders saved in Client Hub. Your store on Uber Eats is not touched. To take
back the access you granted, also remove Client Hub under your integrations
in Uber Eats Manager.

## Good to know

- Uber Eats only lets a store be linked to one project at a time.
- If you remove Client Hub in Uber Eats Manager, the page says **Access
  removed**. Disconnect and connect again to fix it.
- Menus, hours and accepting orders stay in Uber Eats Manager. Client Hub
  doesn't change them.
