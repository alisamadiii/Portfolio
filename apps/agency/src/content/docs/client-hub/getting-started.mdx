Your Client Hub is one place for everything about your website. You change
the site by chatting with AI, answer new leads by text and email, collect
Google reviews, send newsletters and see your traffic. No code, and no
separate logins for five different services.

This guide walks through the Hub with a sample business, Sunny Side Plumbing.
Your screens show your own site and data.

## Signing in

1. Go to [app.alisamadii.com](https://app.alisamadii.com).
2. Sign in with the email we set up together. You can get a sign-in link by
   email, use a password, or continue with Google or GitHub.
3. You land on your site's **Home**. If you have several sites, the Hub opens
   the one you used last and remembers it for next time.

The site switcher at the top left shows which site you are working on. Click
it to jump to another site, to **Import site** (a GitHub repository or an
uploaded folder) or to open **GitHub access**. **Sites** in the sidebar lists
every website you have access to, with its plan and live status.

## Your site's Home page

Every site opens on **Home**. At the top you see your plan, whether the
website is online, and how many apps are installed. Then comes your
dashboard: one section per app that has numbers to show, for the period you
pick at the top right (last 7, 28 or 90 days).

- **Leads**: how many came in, how many are still waiting for a reply, what
  you won, a chart per day and the five latest leads.
- **Analytics**: visitors, sessions, pages per session, time on site, the
  traffic chart and your busiest pages (once Google Analytics is connected).
- **Orders**: orders, sales, average order and estimated payout from your
  delivery apps, with the latest orders. See [Orders](/docs/client-hub/orders).
- **Reviews**: your Google rating and review count, and your Feedback
  responses.

Below the dashboard:

- **Edit with AI** opens the website editor. See
  [Editing Your Site with AI](/docs/client-hub/ai-editing).
- **View site** opens your live website in a new tab.
- **Import** replaces your site's files with a new version (uploaded sites only).
- **GitHub** (site owners, uploaded sites only) shows the private repository
  where every published version of your site is kept, with its latest commit.
  If the code was changed on GitHub, **Pull latest commit** makes the editor
  workspace that exact commit. Like Import, your live site keeps the old
  version until you redeploy.
- **Getting started** is a short checklist: website live, domain connected,
  business info filled in, and so on. Tick through it once and you're set up.

## The sidebar

The left sidebar is the map of your site. It has three parts.

**Your site**

- **Home**: the overview above.
- **Website**: the AI editor with a live preview of your site.
- **Business info**: the facts about your business (name, address, phone,
  services, audience). The AI, your ads and your review page all read from it.
  See [Settings & Account](/docs/client-hub/settings).
- **Templates**: reusable messages and appointment types for your leads.
- **Members**: who can open this site and what they can do (owners and full
  access members only).
- **Netlify**: your hosting status, shown once an uploaded site is published.

**Apps**

The apps you added to this site, like Leads, Analytics or Feedback, followed
by **Add apps**. See [Apps](/docs/client-hub/apps).

**Settings group**

- **Speed**: a speed test of your live site (owners and full access members).
- **Billing**: this site's plan and invoices (owners and full access members).
- **Settings**: your site's name, logo, website address, where form emails go,
  allowed domains, and deleting the site (owner only).

Below your site's pages, the same sidebar shows your account: **Sites**,
**Account**, **Integrations** (Google, GitHub and Netlify connections used
across your sites), **Billing** (your invoices and one-time purchases),
**Partner Program** and **Guide** (this documentation). Switching sites keeps
you on the page you are on.

## Plans

Previewing your site in the Hub is free. Your site's monthly plan unlocks
editing with AI, publishing, and every app. A site without a plan can't add
apps. If something shows a lock, open **Billing** for that site.

## Who can access your site

You own your site, and you decide who else gets in. Open **Members** to invite
people by email and pick a role:

| Role | What they can do |
| --- | --- |
| Full access | Everything, including members, billing and publishing. |
| Content editor | Edit the website and content. No members or billing. |
| View only | See the project. No changes. |

Our team shows up in the list with an **Agency** badge so we can help you.
To hand the whole site to someone else, open the **...** menu on their row and
choose **Make owner**.
