Your Client Hub is one dashboard for everything about your website: editing
content, watching traffic, seeing the emails we send on your behalf, and
checking how fast your site loads. No code, no separate logins for five
different services.

## Logging in

1. Go to [hub.alisamadii.com](https://hub.alisamadii.com).
2. Sign in with the email address we set up together during onboarding.
3. You land on your project dashboard, with one card per website we manage
   for you.

## A quick tour

Once you open your project, the tabs across the top are:

- **Edit**: change text and images on your live site visually. See
  [Editing Your Site with AI](/docs/client-hub/ai-editing) for the fastest way
  to make changes.
- ~~**Blog**: write and publish blog posts without touching code.~~ *(deprecated, being removed)*
- **Analytics**: your Google Analytics traffic, right in the dashboard.
- **Emails**: every email sent from your website (contact-form
  confirmations, newsletters) with delivery status.
- **Domains**: the web addresses connected to your site.
- **Billing**: your plan and invoices.

## Who can access it

You own your account, and you decide who else gets in. Want a team member to
help manage content? Ask us and we'll add them. They get their own login, and
you can remove them any time.

## Something looks wrong?

Every change made through the Hub is version-controlled, which means nothing
is ever lost. Any change can be undone. If something looks broken, just
[contact us](/#contact) and we'll roll it back.
