**Site Monitor** watches your live website around the clock. Open it from the
sidebar in Client Hub.

## Turning it on

If your project already has a website address, press **Start monitoring**.
If it doesn't, type your address (for example `www.example.com`) and press
**Start monitoring**: we save it on your project and start checking it in
one step. Only the project owner can add or change the address.

The first full check starts right away and takes a few minutes.

## What you see

- **Dashboard**: whether your site is up right now, when we last checked,
  a strip of the last 24 hours, uptime for the last 24 hours, 7 days and
  30 days, response times, and when your SSL certificate and domain expire.
- **Problems**: errors and warnings we found, such as broken pages, dead
  links, slow pages, SEO issues or missing email records. Each one shows the
  page it's on and how long it has been there.
- **Pages**: every page we found on your site, with its problems from the
  latest check.
- **History**: every full check, with how many pages were checked and how
  many problems were found.

## Fixing problems

**Copy for AI** copies a problem (or **Copy all for AI** for the whole list)
with the details an AI assistant or developer needs to fix it. Paste it into
your AI chat or send it to whoever looks after your website's code.

If a warning doesn't matter to you, press the bell to **mute** it. Muted
problems stay under **Show muted problems**, where you can un-mute them.

**Run check now** starts a fresh full check instead of waiting for the next
daily one. The result shows up in **History** a few minutes later.
