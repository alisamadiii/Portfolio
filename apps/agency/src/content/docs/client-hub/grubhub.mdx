The **Grubhub** app shows your Grubhub orders next to your website. Staff
confirm, reject and mark orders ready, and pause the store, from any phone.
Orders from every delivery app are also listed together under
[Orders](/docs/client-hub/orders).

## Connecting your store

1. In Client Hub, go to **Apps** and add **Grubhub**.
2. Enter your Grubhub **merchant ID**. It's a number. You find it in Grubhub
   for Restaurants, or ask your Grubhub account manager for it.
3. Client Hub checks the ID with Grubhub and shows the restaurant's name and
   address. Confirm **This is my restaurant**.

The card then says **Waiting for Grubhub**. Grubhub has to switch the
integration on for that store, which we arrange with them. There's nothing to
do on your side. Once it's live, orders arrive within seconds.

## New orders

A new order shows as **New** with a clock. Grubhub cancels an order nobody
confirms within about 8 minutes, so confirm promptly.

- **Confirm order** asks for a **Ready in** time: how long until the food is
  ready. Grubhub shows it to the customer.
- **Reject** is only for orders you can't make at all: closed, item not on the
  menu, price problem, or other with a note. The customer sees the order as
  rejected.

Scheduled orders show as **Scheduled** and become New when it's time to cook.

## While cooking

Press **Ready for pickup** when the food is ready. That covers customer
pickup and Grubhub deliveries. For orders your own drivers deliver, press
**Out for delivery** and then **Delivered**.

For Grubhub-delivered orders the driver is told automatically, and the order
dialog shows when they're expected.

## Cancelling a confirmed order

**Request cancellation** sends a request to Grubhub Care. A person reviews it,
so it isn't instant. If it's approved, the customer is refunded. **Check
status** shows where the request stands. The owner's account does this.

## Pausing

**Go offline** hides your store on Grubhub until you press **Go online**.
**Close for today** closes the store until its next scheduled opening. Both
take an optional reason that only Grubhub sees.

If Grubhub pauses the store itself, for example after missed orders, the card
shows Grubhub's message.

## What the numbers mean

- **Orders today / Orders 7 days / Sales 30 days / Tips 30 days / Open
  orders / Average order**: counted from the orders Client Hub has seen.
- **Sales** is Grubhub's order value before Grubhub's commission, what
  Grubhub calls the merchant total.

Exact payouts and commission are in Grubhub for Restaurants under
**Financials**. Client Hub does not estimate them. Test orders never count.

## Order details and receipt

Click any order to open it: the items with their options and notes, the
customer's name and phone, pickup notes or the delivery address when you
deliver yourself, and the charges. **Print receipt** prints just the receipt.

## Who can do what

- **Owner**: full access. Connects, pauses, disconnects and requests
  cancellations.
- **Staff with edit access**: confirm, reject and mark orders ready.
- **Viewers**: see orders only.

## Disconnecting

**Disconnect and delete saved orders** at the bottom of the store card unlinks
the store and deletes the orders saved in Client Hub. Your store on Grubhub
isn't touched. We ask Grubhub to switch the integration off.

## Good to know

- We save the customer's name and phone with the order for the handoff,
  nothing more.
- The page refreshes itself while it's open.
- Your Grubhub tablet keeps working as a backup.
