Beyond editing, your Client Hub pulls everything about your website into one
place. Here's what each tab does.

## Analytics

Your Google Analytics traffic without logging into Google Analytics. See how
many people visit, which pages they read, and where they come from.

<img
  src="/docs-media/client-hub/analytics.png"

  width="2000"
  height="1255"
  loading="lazy"
/>

Scroll down for the detail: exactly which sources send you traffic (Google,
Instagram, even AI assistants like ChatGPT), which countries your visitors are
in, and your most-visited pages.

## Emails

Every email your website sends (contact-form confirmations, newsletters,
notifications) listed with its delivery status. If a customer says "I never
got a reply," you can check exactly what was sent and when.

<img
  src="/docs-media/client-hub/emails-list.png"
  alt="The Emails tab listing every sent email with delivery status, subject, and date"
  width="2000"
  height="1255"
  loading="lazy"
/>

Click any email to see its full detail: sender, recipient, delivery status,
and a preview of exactly what was sent.

## Speed test

Run a Google Lighthouse speed test on your live site any time and see the
scores for yourself: performance, accessibility, best practices, and SEO. We
build every site to score high; this tab is how you hold us to it.

<img
  src="/docs-media/client-hub/speed.png"
  alt="The Speed tab showing Google Lighthouse scores and Core Web Vitals for mobile and desktop"
  width="2000"
  height="1255"
  loading="lazy"
/>

## Domains

The web addresses connected to your site. See which domain is primary and
what's pointed where. Useful when you add a second domain or move things
around.

## Billing

Your plan, payment method, and invoice history. No surprises: what you pay
and what it covers, in one place.
