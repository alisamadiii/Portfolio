Apps add tools to your site. Each one gets its own page in the sidebar, under
**Apps**. Your site's monthly plan includes every app, so add the ones you use
and skip the rest.

## Adding and removing apps

Click **Add apps** at the bottom of the Apps list in the sidebar.

- Filter by **All**, **Installed** or **In your plan**.
- Click **Add to site** on an app. It appears in the sidebar and opens right away.
- To remove one, open the **...** menu on its card and pick **Remove from site**.
  Removing an app only hides it from the sidebar.
- A locked app means the site has no plan yet. The card links to **Billing**.

## Leads

Every form submission and manual lead in one inbox, with where each lead came
from. Reply by text or email right from the Hub. Leads has its own guide:
[Leads & Conversations](/docs/client-hub/leads).

## Feedback

One review link for your customers. Happy customers are sent to your Google
review page, everyone else tells you privately. See
[Feedback & Google Reviews](/docs/client-hub/feedback).

## Analytics

Your Google Analytics numbers without logging into Google Analytics: visitors,
sessions, pages per session, time on site, a traffic chart, your most-viewed
pages, every event Google recorded (page views, clicks, form submits and any
custom event) and where visitors come from. Tabs: **Overview**, **Pages**,
**Events** and **Sources**.

To connect it, click **Sign in with Google** and choose your site's Analytics
property. Access is read-only: the Hub never changes anything in your Google
account. You can manage the connection under **Integrations**.

## Google Ads

What your ads cost, what they bring in and where the budget goes. Tabs:

- **Overview**: spend and how each campaign performs.
- **Budget**: how the month's spend is pacing.
- **Search terms**: what people actually searched before clicking your ad.
- **Conversions**: whether your lead tracking is working.

We link your Google Ads account for you. Until then the page says "No Google
Ads account linked yet" with a button to contact us.

## Newsletter

Email your customers from your own list, with your branding. Click **Set up
newsletter** once and the Hub creates a private newsletter space for your
site. Then:

- **Campaigns**: write and send emails with a visual editor.
- **Contacts**: your subscriber lists, with CSV import.
- **Templates**: reusable email designs.
- **Suppressions**: people who unsubscribed or bounced. They never get emails.
- **Domain**: send from your own domain so emails land in the inbox.

Nothing sends until you press send on a campaign.

## Resend

If your website sends email through Resend (contact form confirmations, for
example), connect your Resend account here to see your sending domains and the
emails your site sent, and to create API keys.

## Google Calendar

Connect Google to see your calendars and upcoming appointments in a week view,
next to your leads.

## Neon

For sites with a database on Neon: connect your Neon account to see your
projects, branches and connection details.

## Wix

For sites built on Wix: install the Hub on your Wix site to manage its blog,
form leads, contacts, orders and bookings from here. Wix form submissions show
up in Leads.

## Uber Eats

For restaurants on Uber Eats: connect your store to see orders and sales here
and pause orders on busy nights. You keep accepting orders on your Uber Eats
tablet, or accept them in Client Hub. Orders also appear on Home and under
**Orders**. See [Uber Eats orders in Client Hub](/docs/client-hub/uber-eats)
and [Orders](/docs/client-hub/orders).

## DoorDash

For restaurants on DoorDash: sign in with your DoorDash account, pick the
store, and Client Hub asks DoorDash to connect it. The page follows the
request step by step. See [Connecting your DoorDash store](/docs/client-hub/doordash).

## Grubhub

For restaurants on Grubhub: enter your merchant ID, confirm the restaurant,
and we arrange the switch-on with Grubhub. Staff then confirm, reject and mark
orders ready from any phone, and the owner can pause the store. See
[Grubhub orders in Client Hub](/docs/client-hub/grubhub).
