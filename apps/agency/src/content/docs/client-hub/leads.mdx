**Leads** collects every person who contacts you through your website, plus
anyone you add by hand. For each lead you see what they asked, where they came
from (Google Ads, Google search, social, a referral) and where they are in your
process. You can answer them by text or email without leaving the Hub.

The tabs across the top are **Conversations**, **Overview**, **All leads**,
**Sources** and **Settings**.

## Conversations

Leads opens on Conversations: a list of leads on the left, the conversation in
the middle and the lead's details on the right.

- **Filter** by All, Unread or Starred, or search by name, email or phone.
- The form the lead filled in appears at the top of the thread.
- **Reply** at the bottom. Pick **Text** or **Email**, type, and send. Texts
  come from your business texting number, and replies land right back here.
  Emails come from your business, and email replies come back here too.
- Use the **star** to mark leads you want to follow up on, and the phone
  button to call.

On the right, **Lead details** shows:

- **Contact**: name, email and phone (click **Edit** to fix typos).
- **Status**: New, Contacted, Qualified, Won, Lost or Spam. Mark a lead **Won**
  and add the job value to track your revenue.
- **Appointment**: schedule a visit or call.
- **How they found you**: the form, the source (like Google Ads) and when they
  came in.
- **Notes**: private notes only your team sees.

Messages are only sent when you press send. The Hub never texts or emails your
leads on its own.

## Overview

A quick read on how your website is doing at bringing in work: total leads,
leads from ads, organic leads, ad spend, cost per ad lead and won revenue for
the period you pick, plus your latest leads. Numbers we can't measure yet
(like ad spend without a linked ads account) show as a dash.

## Sources

Which channels bring you leads: Google Ads, organic search, social, email,
referrals and direct visits. Use it to see what's worth your money.

## Adding a lead by hand

Got a phone call or a walk-in? Click **Add lead** at the top right so every
customer is tracked in one place.

## Settings

- **Texting**: your business texting number. We set it up for you.
- **Lead capture setup**: the small script and form settings that send your
  website's forms to Leads. We install this on your site, so there's nothing
  for you to do. If you have a developer, they can follow the steps shown here.

## Templates

reusable text snippets with placeholders like the lead's name, then type **/**
in a reply to drop one in. Appointment templates pre-fill the title, length and
notes when you schedule a lead.
