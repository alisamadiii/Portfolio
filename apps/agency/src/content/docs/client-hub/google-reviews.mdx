The **Google Reviews** app keeps a copy of your Google reviews and puts the
best ones on your website. Every review is saved, but your website only shows
**5-star reviews with a written comment**, so every review your visitors see
is a good one.

## Setting it up

1. Open your business on Google Maps, tap **Share** and copy the link.
2. In Client Hub, go to **Apps**, add **Google Reviews**, paste the link and
   press **Connect**.

Right away we save your **50 newest reviews**. After that we check Google
once a day and add any new ones. Reviews are never removed on their own.

## Choosing what shows

Reviews marked **On website** are the ones your site shows. Each has an eye
button: press it to hide that review from your website. It stays in your
list, so you can show it again later.

**Sync now** checks Google straight away instead of waiting for the daily
check. **Change listing** connects a different Google listing; reviews from the
old one are removed.

## Adding reviews to your website

Whoever looks after your website's code adds one line to the page:

```html
<script src="https://api.alisamadii.com/reviews.js" data-key="YOUR_PROJECT_ID" defer></script>
```

Client Hub shows this line with your project already filled in. It only works
on your own website's domains, so nobody can copy your reviews onto another
site.

Then they mark where the reviews go. Each review is a copy of the template,
styled to match your site:

```html
<p><span data-reviews-rating></span> stars from <span data-reviews-count></span> Google reviews</p>
<div class="reviews">
  <template data-review-template>
    <figure>
      <span data-review="stars"></span>
      <p data-review="text"></p>
      <figcaption><span data-review="author"></span>, <time data-review="date"></time></figcaption>
    </figure>
  </template>
</div>
<a data-reviews-write>Leave us a review</a>
```

| Attribute | Fills in |
| --- | --- |
| `data-reviews-rating` | Your Google rating, like 4.9 |
| `data-reviews-count` | How many reviews you have on Google |
| `data-reviews-name` | Your business name |
| `data-reviews-write` (on a link) | Google's "write a review" page |
| `data-reviews-maps` (on a link) | Your Google listing |
| `data-review="author"` | Reviewer's name |
| `data-review="text"` | The review |
| `data-review="stars"` | ★★★★★ |
| `data-review="date"` | Month and year it was posted |
| `data-review="photo"` (on an image) | Reviewer's Google photo |
| `data-review="reply"` | Your reply on Google, if any |
| `data-review="link"` (on a link) | The review on Google |

Add `data-limit="6"` to the script tag to load fewer reviews (up to 50).
Developers who build their own layout can listen for the `reviews:load` event
on `document` instead; its `detail` holds the business info and the reviews.
