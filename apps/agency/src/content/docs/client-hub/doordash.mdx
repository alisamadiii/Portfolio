The **DoorDash** app connects your DoorDash store to Client Hub. You sign in
with DoorDash once, pick the store, and DoorDash sets up the connection on its
side. The page shows every step as it happens, in plain words.

Right now the app covers the connection itself. Menus and orders stay in the
DoorDash Merchant Portal and on your tablet; they come to Client Hub in a later
update.

## Connecting your store

1. In Client Hub, go to **Apps** and add **DoorDash**.
2. Press **Connect DoorDash** and sign in with the DoorDash account that
   manages your restaurant. DoorDash asks whether Client Hub may see your
   stores. You never share your password with us.
3. Pick the store this website is for. If your account manages several, each
   store connects to its own project.
4. Optionally, tell us who signs off on the connection at the restaurant.
   DoorDash emails that person if it needs a decision.

DoorDash needs the account you sign in with to be a **business admin** for
every store on the account. If it isn't, the page tells you, and someone with
that role has to connect instead.

New to DoorDash? The **Sign up** link takes you to DoorDash's merchant
sign-up. Come back and connect once your store exists there.

## Already using another integration?

If your store already sends DoorDash orders to a POS or another integration,
connecting it here **replaces that integration once the connection goes
live**. The page says so before you pick a store. If you're not sure, ask us
before connecting.

## What the status means

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Requested | We asked DoorDash to connect the store. The first update usually arrives within minutes. |
| Fetching menu | DoorDash is pulling the store's menu. |
| Waiting on menu | DoorDash couldn't get a menu, so the connection is on hold. In this version of the app that's expected: menus aren't available from Client Hub yet, and there's nothing to do on your side. |
| In review | DoorDash is setting the store up. |
| Blocked | DoorDash can't activate the store yet. The note under the status says why, for example missing store hours or banking details in the Merchant Portal. |
| Menu ready | DoorDash accepted the menu. Activation is next. |
| Live | The store is connected and DoorDash sends its orders through this integration. |
| Abandoned | DoorDash closed the request. Press **Restart** to ask again. |

Updates arrive on their own. **Refresh** asks DoorDash for the latest status
right away.

## Restarting

**Restart** asks DoorDash to start the request over, for example after you
fixed something in the Merchant Portal. DoorDash needs you to sign in again
for that, so the button opens the DoorDash sign-in and brings you straight
back. Restart isn't available while DoorDash is still fetching the menu.

## Disconnecting

**Disconnect** unlinks the store from this site. Your store on DoorDash isn't
touched. If DoorDash was still working on a request, it finishes or abandons
it on its own.

## Good to know

- DoorDash lets a store connect to one project at a time, and a project holds
  one DoorDash store.
- Stores shown greyed out as **Not eligible** can't be connected right now.
  DoorDash decides that; its merchant support can tell you why.
- Menus, hours and store availability stay in the DoorDash Merchant Portal.
  Client Hub doesn't change them.
