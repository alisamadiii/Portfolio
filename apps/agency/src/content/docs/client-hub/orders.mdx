**Orders** collects the orders from every delivery app you have connected
into one list, newest first. It appears in the sidebar as soon as you add
Uber Eats or DoorDash.

## What you see

- Four numbers for the chosen period: orders, sales, average order and the
  estimated payout (once you have entered your commission rate in the Uber
  Eats app).
- The order list: which app it came from, order number and customer first
  name, when it was placed in your store's local time, the items, the total
  and its status.
- Filters: **All apps** or one app, and **All**, **Open**, **Completed** or
  **Canceled**. The period switcher at the top right covers the last 7, 28
  or 90 days.

Click an order to open its details: every item with its options, the
customer's note, the charges, the courier when Uber delivers, and a printable
receipt. This is the same dialog as in the Uber Eats app.

If your store accepts orders in Client Hub, new orders show an **Accept**
button right in the list.

## Home

Home shows the same numbers and the five latest orders for the chosen
period, so you see how the day is going without opening the page.

## Which apps send orders

- **Uber Eats**: every order, within a minute of being placed. See
  [Uber Eats orders in Client Hub](/docs/client-hub/uber-eats).
- **DoorDash**: the store connection is in place today; orders come to Client
  Hub once DoorDash turns on order sharing for the integration. See
  [Connecting your DoorDash store](/docs/client-hub/doordash).
