**Feedback** gives you one link to send customers after a job. They tap a star
rating. Happy customers (5 stars) are asked to post their review on Google.
Anyone less happy gets a private form, so you hear about problems first and can
fix them.

## Your review page

Your link looks like `www.alisamadii.com/review/SunnyPb7` and opens a simple
page with your logo, business name and address. It works on any phone, no app
or login needed.

- **5 stars:** "Thank you, that means a lot!" and a **Post your review on
  Google** button that opens your Google review form.
- **1 to 4 stars:** "We're sorry it wasn't perfect" and a private form for what
  could be better, with an optional name and email or phone. It goes only to
  you, in the Feedback app.

## Setting it up

1. **Add your Google listing in Settings.** Under **Google listing**, search
   the name exactly as it shows on Google, plus your city, and pick your
   business listing (not just the street address). Your business name,
   address, phone and hours are filled in from Google.
2. **Check your business details in Settings.** The review page shows your
   business name and address, so both are required. Add your site logo too,
   so customers recognize you.
3. Open **Feedback** and press **Create review link**.
4. **Paste your Google review link.** In your
   [Google Business Profile](https://business.google.com/), select **Read
   reviews**, then **Get more reviews**, then **Share**, and copy the link. It
   looks like `https://g.page/r/.../review`.
   Your review link goes live once this is pasted.
5. **Copy your review link** and start sharing it.

## Sharing the link

Text it after each job, add it to your invoices and email signature, or put it
on a card or QR code at the counter. Use **Test it** next to the Google review
link to check that it opens your Google review form.

## Reading your feedback

The Feedback page shows:

- **Ratings**: how many people rated you.
- **Average**: your average star rating.
- **Sent to Google**: how many were asked to post on Google.
- **Private feedback**: how many left a private message.

The table below lists every rating with the message, name and date. Filter by
5 stars, 4, 3, or 1 to 2. Reach out to unhappy customers quickly: a fast fix
often turns them into fans.

To point the link at a different Google listing, click **Change business**.
