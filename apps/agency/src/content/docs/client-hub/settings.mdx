Everything that isn't day-to-day work lives here: the facts about your
business, who has access, your site's settings and plan, and your account.

## Business info

Seven short sections about your business: what you sell, who your customers
are, the problem you solve, your taglines and brand purpose, plus your Google
Ads keywords. Answer them once in your own words. We use them for your website
copy, your Google Ads and your emails, and your review page reads your business
name, address and phone from here. The logo is your site logo from
[Settings](#settings), shown here too.

Stuck on a section? Each one has a ready-made AI prompt. Copy it into ChatGPT,
then paste back the answers you like. The progress bar at the top shows what's
left and what to do next.

## Settings

Open **Settings** at the bottom of the sidebar. Only the site's owner (and our
team) can see it.

- **Site name**: the name shown in the site switcher and your Sites list. Leave
  it empty to use your website address.
- **Site logo**: one logo for your site, shown on your review page, in the site
  switcher and your Sites list. The same logo card also appears in Business
  info, so changing it in either place changes it everywhere.
- **Website address**: where your live site is, like `https://www.yourbusiness.com`.
  The speed test and search settings use it.
- **Notification email**: where new lead and form emails go. Leave it empty to
  use the account email.
- **Allowed domains**: your lead forms only work on the domains listed here, so
  nobody can send fake leads from another site.
- **Delete this site**: removes the site and its settings, members and data
  from the Hub for good. It can't be undone, so contact us first if you're
  unsure.

## Netlify

Uploaded sites are hosted on your own Netlify account. Once you publish, a
**Netlify** page appears in the sidebar with your live address, the latest
build, recent deploys and your domains. If a deploy fails, use **Redeploy**
there.

## Integrations

Open **Integrations** from the account sidebar (no site selected). It lists the
accounts the Hub uses across all your sites, like GitHub, Google Analytics and
Netlify, with a button to connect or manage each one.

**Account** holds your profile. The account-level
**Billing** page shows all your plans and purchases in one place.

## Partner Program

Know other business owners who need a website? Refer them and earn 40% of
their first website payment, after payment processing fees. Open **Partner
Program** in the account sidebar to apply. Once approved, the same page shows
your referral code, the businesses you referred and your payouts.
