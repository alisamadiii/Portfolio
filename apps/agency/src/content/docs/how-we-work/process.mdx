You may have seen the "How We Work" animation on our homepage. This is the
same story, slowed down: every step, and why we do it this way.

The short version: **you own everything.** The accounts, the code, the domain.
We build and maintain; you hold the keys.

## Step 1: One shared email, set up together

We start from a single email address. It can be yours, or one we create
together on our domain: `yourname@alihq.org`. That email is used to create
every account your website depends on:

- **Proton Pass**, an encrypted vault holding every password and secret key
- **GitHub**, where your website's code lives
- **Netlify**, the engine that hosts and serves your site

Because the accounts are created on *your* email, they are yours from day
one. We get developer access to do our work, and you can revoke that access
whenever you want.

## Step 2: Your code lives in your GitHub

Everything we build is saved to a GitHub repository owned by your account.
Every page, every edit, every fix: recorded, timestamped, undoable. Nothing
exists only on our computers.

If we disappeared tomorrow, your complete website is sitting in your GitHub,
and any developer in the world could pick it up.

## Step 3: Netlify runs the engine

Netlify connects to your GitHub and does the serving: your domain, DNS,
hosting, and assets. Fast, secure, and free for most local businesses.

When code changes in GitHub, Netlify notices, rebuilds, and publishes the
live site automatically, usually within a minute. There is no manual
"publish to the server" step, and no server for you to maintain.

```
You edit content  ->  saved to GitHub  ->  Netlify rebuilds  ->  live site updates
```

## Step 4: Your Client Hub does the rest

Day to day, you don't touch GitHub or Netlify. You use your
[Client Hub](/docs/client-hub/getting-started): edit your site without
touching code, watch your Google Analytics traffic, see every email we send,
run a speed test. One dashboard.

## Step 5: You stay in control

This structure has one purpose: **you are never locked out of your own
business.**

- Passwords live in your Proton Pass vault, not in our heads.
- The code lives in your GitHub, not on our laptop.
- The hosting runs on your Netlify account, not our server.
- Everything is documented in a handoff document you keep. See
  [Your Handoff Document](/docs/ownership/client-handoff).

You can fire us, outlive us, or replace us, and your website keeps running.
That's the point.
