FAQ

Newsletter questions

What does it cost?

A flat monthly amount quoted for your business — never a per-contact fee. For a growing list it's a fraction of Mailchimp or Klaviyo, and you get the price in writing before you commit.

Do I have to write the newsletter myself?

No. The editor is simple — or send us the news and we write, design, and send it.

Can I bring my existing contact list?

Yes. Spreadsheets, exports from other tools, even loyalty-card emails — imported and cleaned during setup.

Is this just Mailchimp under the hood?

No. It's a private platform we run, branded for your business and sending from your domain — no third-party logo, no shared address.

Who owns the subscriber list?

You do. Export it with one click — it stays yours even if you cancel.
