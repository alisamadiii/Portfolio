The checklist

What a pest control website actually needs

Most pest control sites are a logo, a phone number in the footer, and a photo of a handshake. These six things turn a visitor into a booked job — and every site we build ships with all of them.

✆

Click-to-call everywhere

A sticky call bar on mobile and your number in every section. Pest problems are emergencies — the visitor who has to hunt for your number calls someone else.

⌖

Service-area pages

One page per city you cover, each built to rank for "pest control near me." That's how pest control companies show up without buying ads.

★

A review wall

Your Google reviews, pulled onto the site front and center. Nobody lets a stranger into their house without checking what the neighbors said.

▤

Booking and quote form

A short form that lands in your inbox with the pest, the address, and a photo. Quote from your truck, not from voicemail tag.

◐

Before-and-after proof

Photos of real jobs: the termite damage, the treated baseboards, the sealed entry points. Proof beats promises on every service page.

✓

License and trust badges

State license number, insurance, guarantees, association logos — what separates a real operator from a guy with a sprayer and a magnet sign.
