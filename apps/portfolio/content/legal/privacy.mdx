**Last updated: October 25, 2026**

AliSamadii.LLC ("we," "our," or "us") operates the websites alisamadii.com, motion.alisamadii.com, docs.alisamadii.com, and packages.alisamadii.com (the "Services"). This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our Services, including when you sign in with Google or other third-party providers.

## Information We Collect

We may collect information that you provide directly (e.g., when you contact us or create an account) and information we obtain when you use our Services:

- **Account information:** If you sign in with Google or another provider, we receive your name, email address, and profile picture (or other profile data made available by the provider) to create and manage your account.
- **Usage data:** We may collect information about how you use our Services (e.g., pages visited, features used).
- **Device and log data:** We may collect IP address, browser type, and similar technical information.

## How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

- Provide, maintain, and improve our Services
- Authenticate you and manage your account
- Send you service-related communications (e.g., password reset, security notices)
- Respond to your requests and support you
- Comply with legal obligations and protect our rights

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

## Google User Data

Some of our Services connect to Google APIs with your permission, such as Sign in with Google, Google Ads, and Google Calendar. When you grant access, we may receive:

- **Google account profile:** your name, email address, and profile picture, used to create and manage your account.
- **Google Ads data:** campaign, conversion, and reporting data from Google Ads accounts you authorize, used solely to provide advertising management, reporting, and conversion tracking features you request.
- **Google Calendar data:** calendar events and free/busy availability from calendars you authorize, used solely to show availability and create bookings you request.

We use Google user data only to provide and improve the user-facing features described above. We do not use Google user data for advertising purposes of our own, and we do not sell it. We do not transfer Google user data to third parties except as necessary to provide these features (e.g., our hosting and database providers), to comply with applicable law, or as part of a merger or acquisition with prior notice to you.

Our use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the [Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy), including the Limited Use requirements.

You can revoke our access to your Google data at any time from your [Google Account security settings](https://myaccount.google.com/permissions), or by disconnecting the integration inside the Services. You may also request deletion of your stored Google user data by contacting us at the email below; we will delete it within 30 days.

## Cookies and Similar Technologies

We use cookies and similar technologies to keep you signed in, remember your preferences, and understand how our Services are used. You can control cookies through your browser settings.

## Third-Party Services

Our Services may use third-party services (e.g., Google for sign-in, analytics, hosting). Those services have their own privacy policies governing how they collect and use data. We encourage you to review Google's Privacy Policy when you use "Sign in with Google."

## Data Retention and Security

We retain your information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the Services and fulfill the purposes described in this policy. When you delete your account or disconnect an integration, we delete the associated data, including Google user data and OAuth tokens, within 30 days, except where retention is required by law.

We protect your data, including sensitive data received from Google APIs, with the following mechanisms:

- **Encryption in transit:** all data is transmitted over HTTPS using TLS 1.2 or higher.
- **Encryption at rest:** our databases and backups are encrypted at rest.
- **Access controls:** access to personal and sensitive data is restricted to authorized personnel who need it to operate the Services, and OAuth tokens are stored server-side and are never exposed to other users or third parties.
- **Credential security:** API credentials and OAuth refresh tokens are stored in secured environment configuration, not in client-side code.
- **Monitoring and revocation:** we promptly revoke stored tokens when you disconnect an integration or delete your account.

No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we review and update these safeguards on an ongoing basis.

## Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete your personal information, or to object to or restrict certain processing. To exercise these rights or ask questions about your data, contact us at the email below.

## Children's Privacy
