How this gets used: approve this page, then press Copy full kit (in Build prompt) and paste it into a new Claude chat with the launch-motion skill loaded. The video is animated in code (Remotion) from these SVGs and rendered on your Mac, which costs no credits. Higgsfield is only used for the music, a few sound effects and, if you want them, 2 background images. Never ask Higgsfield to generate the video itself: AI video models scramble UI text and every retry costs credits.

Brief

launch-motion brief template
Company
Ali Samadii LLC designs and hand-codes custom websites for local businesses, built on the client's own Shopify, Wix or Square, and gives them Client Hub: one place to edit the site, publish it, answer leads, collect reviews, send newsletters, see orders and analytics, and watch uptime.
Audience
Owners of local businesses (restaurants, cafes, shops, service businesses) who run their business from their phone and too many tabs.
Goal
Hero video on alisamadii.com (autoplay, muted, looping), also usable on social with sound.
One message
A custom website on your platform, plus everything to run it, in one hub.
Must show
  1. The hook: too many tabs to run one small business.
  2. The product: Client Hub, one window.
  3. The key promise: a custom-designed site while Shopify, Wix or Square keeps running the store, bookings and checkout.
  4. The features working: AI edits, publish to Netlify, code you own, leads + SMS, Google Reviews, newsletter, orders, analytics, site monitor.
  5. Payoff: every app in one hub, logo, alisamadii.com.
Tone
Calm, warm, premium. Clean product UI on a light warm canvas, coral accents. Not cartoonish, no stock footage, no voiceover. Music bed + light SFX.
Deliverables
60 s master 1920 × 1080, 60 fps, a full-quality master mp4 (H.264, CRF ~16, AAC 192 kbps) plus web encodes for the hero, mp4 and VP9 webm, each under 8 MB; poster.webp from frame 3480 (tagline); optional 15 s cutdown (scenes 1, 2, 3, 12).
Demo data
Fictional business Harbor Coffee Co. No real client names. Every number is sample data and must look plausible, not hyped.
Out of scope
DoorDash and Grubhub (still "coming soon"). Google Ads (left out for now). Booking, calls, ad campaigns and reports (GoHighLevel handles them).

Brand

from apps/hub/app/tokens.css and the agency homepage hero

Type

  • Plus Jakarta Sans 800: headlines and captions (matches the homepage H1). Letter-spacing −0.04em.
  • Inter 400–700: all hub UI text (the real hub font).
  • Fragment Mono: platform pills only (matches the homepage platform cards).
  • All three from Google Fonts. Load them before frame 0 or the render falls back to Times.

UI rules

  • Coral fills always carry ink text (#221f1c), never white.
  • Cards: 1px #e7e3de border, radius 10, no shadow. Buttons radius 6, never pills.
  • Hub window floats on the warm canvas with one soft shadow (window only).
  • Key text at least 28 px on the 1920 canvas so it reads at hero size (about 1100 px wide).
  • Logo: the Ali Samadii mark from packages/ui/src/icons/logo.tsx.

Storyboard

source of truth: never reorder, add or drop scenes

SVG assets

Every asset is a standalone SVG drawn for the 1920 × 1080 canvas at scale 1. Animated parts are groups with ids (for example tab-3, send-btn, bar-12) so Claude can animate each one. Text stays live <text> so copy changes are one-line edits. No raster images anywhere.

Sound

music bed + 8 reusable SFX, placed by frame

Music bed (generate once)


        
One take. Re-roll only if the loop point clicks or the drop misses ~7 s. Trim or time-stretch in code so the drop lands on frame 420 and the lift on 3180.

      

      

        
Mix rules

        
    
          
  • Music bed around −18 LUFS; ducks ~4 dB for 200 ms under each SFX hit.
    • 
          
  • The beat drop lands on frame 420 and the lift on frame 3180. Trim or time-stretch the track to fit.
    • 
          
  • SFX are short (under 400 ms), except the type.wav loop, which runs at low volume for as long as the typing lasts.
    • 
          
  • Never more than 2 SFX on the same frame. Staggered pops alternate every other card.
    • 
          
  • Pops pitch up one semitone per staggered item (playbackRate 1.06ⁿ) so lists feel alive, not repetitive.
    • 
          
  • Loop: music fades down to the opening pad over frames 3540–3599 and fades up over frames 0–30, so the 60 s loop has no click.
    • 
          
  • No voiceover. Export AAC 192 kbps in the mp4; the webm carries Opus.
